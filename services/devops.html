DevSecOps vs SecOps

While DevSecOps focuses deeply on natively integrating security fundamentally into the rapid software development lifecycle itself, SecOps focuses sharply on aggressively managing the ongoing continuous security of live IT operations.

Together, they result in drastically faster, completely secure software delivery with dramatically reduced security risk margins.
